Method
  • Forecast cells: high or low temperature over chance of precipitation, Thursday through Sunday night, from the NWS point forecast at the trailhead or a grid cell near the high point. Refreshed live on load; the snapshot is from Sept 14.
  • Limits: every alternative is 30 miles or less with 5,000 ft of gain or less. Plan A is on the board for comparison at 5,500 ft.
  • Arrive: departure plus drive, no stops. Green is before 6 pm, amber is by sunset (about 7:30), red is after dark.
  • Weather rating: 0–10 from the Friday through Sunday night forecast at the high point. Average chance of precip is the main term; any period at 65% or more, a low under 28°F, or a snow mention costs extra points.
  • Drive: highway estimates from SLC airport and Pocatello, dirt not included. Southern Utah is 6–8.5 h from Pocatello.
  • Permits: Tetons, Zion, Canyonlands, Cedar Mesa, Paria, and Buckskin cap groups and need reservations or walk-ins. Slot canyons are out on any day with storms in the forecast.
