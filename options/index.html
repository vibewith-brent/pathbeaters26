How we scored

Fit = epicness × weather × logistics × novelty

  • Epicness (40%): gut call, 1–10, for scenery, summit or objective, and the "we did that" factor. Subjective and unapologetic.
  • Weather (30%): from the live NWS high-point forecast, Friday through Sunday night. Average chance of precip is the main term; any period at or above 65% costs extra, any low under 28°F or a snow mention costs a little.
  • Logistics (20%): drive time from SLC, permits or shuttles, and how cleanly it fits arriving Thursday at 6 pm and flying home Monday.
  • Novelty (10%): we've done the Sawtooths four times and the Winds twice; new ranges get the bump.

Distances and gain are from AllTrails or the route's most cited guide and rounded; drive times are highway estimates from SLC airport and don't include the dirt-road crawl at the end. Verify permits before committing; Grand Teton, Zion, Canyonlands, Cedar Mesa, and Paria all cap groups and require reservations or walk-ins. Slot canyons are off the table on any day with storms in the forecast, full stop.
