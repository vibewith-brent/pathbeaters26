Method
  • Forecast: NWS point forecasts for the trailhead and for a grid cell near the route's high point, pulled live when this page loads; the baked-in snapshot is from Sept 14. The weather score uses Friday through Sunday night at the high point: average chance of precip, a penalty for any period at or above 65%, and small penalties for lows under 28°F or a snow mention.
  • Epicness: a 1–10 gut rating for scenery, objective, and the "we did that" factor. Logistics: drive time, permits, shuttles, how cleanly it fits a Thursday 6 pm arrival and a Monday flight. Novelty: we've done the Sawtooths four times and the Winds twice.
  • Stats are rounded from AllTrails or the linked guide; drive times are highway estimates from SLC airport and skip the dirt at the end. Weather Underground links open the nearest town's 10-day page.
  • Permits: Grand Teton, Zion, Canyonlands, Cedar Mesa, Paria, and Buckskin cap groups and need reservations or walk-ins. Slot canyons are off the table on any day with storms in the forecast.
